CLOSET FOR MAC
==============

Closet keeps your clothes, outfits and a calendar of what you wore, all on this Mac.
No account, no sign-up, nothing online. Needs macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer; works on Intel and Apple-chip Macs.


INSTALL (about 2 minutes)
-------------------------
1. Double-click "Closet-1.0.0-universal.dmg". A window opens.
2. Drag the Closet icon onto the Applications folder.
3. The FIRST time only, open it like this (Apple blocks apps that aren't from the App Store
   if you just double-click them):
   - Open Finder > Applications.
   - Right-click (or hold Control and click) Closet, then choose "Open".
   - A warning appears. Click "Open".
   If your Mac only shows "OK" or "Done" (no Open button):
   - Click OK / Done.
   - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, and click
     "Open Anyway" next to "Closet was blocked". Enter your password, then click Open.
   This happens because the app isn't sold through Apple, not because anything is wrong.
4. Closet opens. From now on, open it like any other app.
5. You can eject the "Closet" disk on the desktop and delete the .dmg and .zip files.

STILL WON'T OPEN?
   Open the Terminal app (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste this line and press Return:
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Closet.app
   It removes the "downloaded from the internet" mark from Closet only. Then open Closet again.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
- Profile: type your name and pick a picture and a color.
- Add clothes: choose a photo of one piece of clothing (iPhone HEIC photos work too).
  The background is removed for you. Fix it with Erase / Restore if needed, then Done.
  Add a name and tags (type, color, season...) and Save item.
  Tip: photos on a plain floor, bed or wall cut out best.
- Outfits > New: add pieces from your closet and drag, resize and turn them on the board.
- Today: plan or log what you wear each day, rate it and react (Loved it, Comfy...).
- Calendar: see every day's outfit. Stats: what you wear most and least.


WHERE YOUR CLOSET IS SAVED
--------------------------
Everything stays on this Mac, in:
   (your user folder) > Library > Application Support > Closet > closet
It holds closet.json (your items, outfits, calendar and settings) and a photos folder.

To see it: Profile > Closet file > Show folder.
To keep it somewhere else (e.g. Documents or iCloud Drive): Profile > Closet file >
"Change folder...". Your closet and photos are moved there.
If you use iCloud Drive, only open Closet on one Mac at a time.


KEEP A COPY (do this now and then!)
-----------------------------------
Your closet is only on this Mac. If the Mac is lost or erased, so is your closet.
Profile > Closet file > Export closet saves everything as ONE .zip file.
Put it in iCloud Drive or Google Drive, or email it to yourself.

Moving to a new computer? Install Closet there and use Profile > Closet file >
Import a closet with that .zip file.


UPDATING OR REMOVING
--------------------
Update: install the new version the same way. Your closet is kept.
Remove: drag Closet from Applications to the Trash. Your closet folder is NOT deleted
(delete the folder above too if you want it gone).
